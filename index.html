01 / The short version

From the peloton to planning and Python.

I spent 15 years racing professionally, including the Tour de France, Giro d’Italia and Vuelta a España. That experience taught me to think ahead, work closely with a team and stay calm when plans change.

After racing, I worked in customer service and marketing at ShoreWork Sports, handling customer enquiries, analysing customer feedback, supporting PR and working with cycling teams and athletes on sponsorships. I also coached professional cyclists before joining Specialized Bicycle Company in 2023. There, I moved into logistics, warehouse analysis and costing, building a historical cost database in Snowflake and Tableau dashboards.

Since July 2025, I’ve been a Global Supply Planner for Road, Active and Wheels. I use sales data and forecasts to make decisions on ordering and stock allocations across Europe, the US and Asia. I’m also analysing obsolete inventory and developing a Python-based monthly MOQ analysis. Alongside work, I’m studying Data Science at IU.
